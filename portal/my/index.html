What you'll find inside

Your rent, counting live

Your USD rent ticks up every second, to the eighth decimal, next to what your land earns a day and a month. Below it sit your Ink, pigments, XP and tokens, your daily streak and where you rank among all players.

Where every Ink comes from

Day-by-day charts of the Ink you earned and spent, split by source: plot minigames, the arcade, the farm, Inkborn, the daily wheel and more. Pick 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or a year, and switch any chart to a table.

The ledger lists every single change to your Ink, pigments, XP and tokens with the reason for it, ready to filter.

All your land on one map

Every plot you own on the world map, down to its real square when you zoom in. Search by name, city, country or type, sort by what each one earns, and open any plot for its full history.

Download the whole list as a spreadsheet file whenever you like.

Your collection, rarity by rarity

Which rarities you own, which you haven't found yet, and what each one earns a day. Plus every plot type, country and city you've collected, and every achievement.

Every day you played

A calendar of your year, your daily and best streaks, how many minigames you've won, and your best score on every level of every minigame.

Questions

What is My LANDVAS?

My LANDVAS is the account page for LANDVAS players. It shows everything about your account in one place: your live USD rent, every plot you own and where it is, every Ink, pigment and XP you've earned or spent, your collection, your activity and your full history.

How do I sign in?

With the same email and password you use in LANDVAS. You can also have a sign-in link sent to your email, or reset your password from the sign-in page.

Is My LANDVAS free?

Yes. Every LANDVAS player can use it at no cost.

Can I change anything here?

No, My LANDVAS is read-only. Claiming plots, buying, painting, playing and cash-outs all happen in the game, and show up here.

How up to date are the numbers?

Your rent counts up live, every second. Everything else refreshes every minute while the page is open.

Who can see my page?

Only you, while you're signed in. Every number on it is loaded for your account alone, and the server checks that on every request.

Can I download my data?

Yes. All my data shows every record LANDVAS keeps about your account, and you can download all of it in one file. Your plot list downloads as a spreadsheet file too.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It's made for phones first, with tabs at the bottom of the screen, and works in any modern browser. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app.
