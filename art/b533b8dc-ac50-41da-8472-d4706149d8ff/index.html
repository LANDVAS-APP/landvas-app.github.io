LANDVAS — Own a piece. Paint the world.

LANDVAS is a free map art game: claim real-world plots, paint pixel art murals the whole world sees on a live map, win minigames for Ink and earn USD rent every second. Your first 4 plots are free.

How LANDVAS works

  • Claim real-world plots. The planet is divided into 4.3 billion house-sized plots. Your first 4 plots are free, and every plot's type and rarity (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic) is a surprise until you claim it.
  • Paint pixel art and murals. Draw or upload artwork with brushes, layers and symmetry, connect neighbouring plots into one continuous canvas, and publish it to the live world map.
  • Win minigames for Ink. Eight plot minigames — TraceCraft, ColorBurst, Paint Pop, LightLines, Memory Mural, Sculptor, Roller Mural and Mirrorline — plus free PvP minigames like Copy Cat pay Ink, the currency for more land, art tools and explorer outfits.
  • Earn USD rent every second. Every plot earns rent by rarity, with a free daily 20× boost. Convert rent to Ink at $1 = 100 Ink from just $0.01.
  • Play together. Visit artists' rooms, chat with the world, add friends and earn Ink for every friend you invite — plus a share of what they earn.
